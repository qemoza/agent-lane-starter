# How we do things, step by step

"Prep me for a call" gets you something generic. "Prep me for a call, and here is
exactly how I prep" gets you your version.

Write down the jobs you repeat. The fastest way: talk one through out loud as if
you were training a new hire, then write down the steps you said.

Keep the shape below. Two or three real ones beat twenty half-written ones.

---

**What you need first:** the inputs, and where to find each one.

**Steps:**

3.

**What good looks like:** describe the finished thing. Paste a real example if you
have one. An example is worth more than the steps.

**Never do:** the mistakes you have already had to correct.
